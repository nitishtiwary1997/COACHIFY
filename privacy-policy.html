Coachify Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how Coachify ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information in the Coachify mobile app ("the App"). Coachify helps coaching institutes manage students, batches, fees, exams, homework and reports.

1. Who controls the data

Each coaching institute that registers on Coachify controls the data of its own students and staff. We process that data on the institute's behalf to provide the App. If you are a student or parent, contact your institute about your data. You may also contact us.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address and password (stored securely by Firebase Authentication).
  • Institute information: institute name, address and contact details entered at registration.
  • Student and staff records: name, phone numbers, father's name, date of birth, gender, photo, courses, batches, admission date, exam marks and homework.
  • Fee records: fee amounts, discounts, dues, payments received and receipts. The App does not process card or bank payments and does not store card numbers.
  • Files: files uploaded as study material or photos.
  • Device and usage data: crash reports, diagnostics, app interaction analytics, push-notification tokens and app-integrity tokens.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the App's features, such as student management, fee collection, receipts, reports and notifications.
  • To secure accounts, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep each institute's data separate.
  • To fix crashes and improve the App.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not show advertisements.

4. Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics and App Check), provided by Google LLC, to host and run the App.

Data is stored on Google Cloud servers, primarily in India (asia-south1).

See Google's privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy

5. Data security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by Google Cloud. Access is restricted by role. Each institute can access only its own data.

6. Data retention and account deletion

We keep data while the institute's account is active. You can request deletion of your account and its data:

  • In the App: More → tap your name at the top → Delete my account (you will be asked for your password).
  • By email: Send a request to info.nitish1997@gmail.com from your registered email address.

We delete the data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.

7. Children

The App is intended for coaching institutes and their staff. Student records, including records of minors, are entered and managed by the institute, which is responsible for obtaining any required consent from parents or guardians.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data by contacting us. We handle requests under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post changes on this page and update the date above.
